How to merge PDF files

  1. Select at least two PDF files from your device.
  2. Check the list. Files will be merged in the displayed order.
  3. Select Merge PDF files and download the combined PDF.

Privacy: PDF contents are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to NoeloTools. Avoid closing this page while the merge is running. Your browser needs an internet connection to load the PDF processing library.
