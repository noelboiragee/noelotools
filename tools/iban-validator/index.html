Money & Tax

IBAN Validator

Validate IBAN structure, country code, country length and MOD-97 checksum. It does not verify account existence.

Use the tool

Spaces are ignored. This is a format and checksum check only.

How to use

  1. Enter the required text or choose an image file.
  2. Select the options you need.
  3. Press Process and review, copy or download the result.

Frequently asked questions

What does this check?

Basic structure, country-specific length and MOD-97 checksum.

Does valid mean the bank account exists?

No. This tool cannot verify account existence, ownership or active status.

Why might a country be unsupported?

Only countries in the built-in length table are checked.
