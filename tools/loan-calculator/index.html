Formula used

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly payments. At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n.

Important: This is an illustrative fixed-rate repayment estimate, not a country-specific legal or APR calculator. Actual APR, lender fees, insurance, taxes, early-repayment charges, affordability checks and disclosure rules vary by country and lender. Use the rate and charges in your lender’s offer.

How to use

  1. Select the currency used in your loan agreement and enter the loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate as a percentage.
  3. Enter the term and choose months or years.
  4. Select Calculate EMI to view the summary and scrollable schedule.

Frequently asked questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment: the regular monthly payment for a fixed-rate repayment loan.

Can I enter a term in months?

Yes. Choose Months or Years from the term unit selector.

Does this calculator follow every European country’s loan regulations?

No. European countries have different consumer-credit rules and lenders have different offers. This tool calculates a fixed-rate repayment estimate; it does not determine legal eligibility, APR disclosures or country-specific fees.

Does the estimate include fees?

No. The calculation excludes lender fees, insurance, taxes, early-repayment charges and variable-rate changes.

How is a 0% rate handled?

The principal is divided equally across all monthly payments.
