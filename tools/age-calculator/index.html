EVERYDAY

Age Calculator

Calculate an exact age in years, months and days, plus the days until the next birthday.

Use the tool

Choose dates from the calendar. Leave “Calculate age on” empty to use today.

How to use

  1. Enter the required values or choose a supported file.
  2. Select the calculation or conversion options.
  3. Press the action button and review the result. Download or copy it where available.

Frequently asked questions

Which date format should I use?

Choose a date from the calendar.

Can I calculate age on a past date?

Yes. Use the optional calculation date field.

Does the birthday countdown include today?

It counts whole days until the next birthday date.
