Unit Converter

Convert length, weight, temperature, volume, area and speed with metric-first units.

Use the tool

How to use

  1. Choose a unit category, such as length, weight or temperature.
  2. Enter the value and select the units to convert from and to.
  3. Select Calculate to see the converted value.

Frequently asked questions

Which unit groups are supported?

Length, weight, temperature, volume, area and speed.

Are metric units prioritised?

Yes. Metric units appear first in the unit lists.

How is temperature converted?

Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin use their standard conversion formulas.
