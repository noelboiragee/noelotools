Money Tax

Sales Tax Calculator

Calculate net, sales tax and gross totals for multiple line items with custom rates and EU reference-rate quick picks.

Use the tool

Enter each amount as net or gross. Reference rates are examples only: check the official rate for your country and product.

How to use

  1. Add a line item for each product or service.
  2. Enter its amount, choose whether it is net or gross, and set the tax rate.
  3. Calculate to see net, tax and gross totals for every line and the complete invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as the VAT Calculator?

No. This tool totals multiple line items, each with its own rate and net-or-gross input basis.

Are the quick-pick rates official for every country?

No. They are common reference rates only. Check the current official rate for the country and product.

Does this calculate business tax liability?

No. It is a general arithmetic aid, not tax or accounting advice.
