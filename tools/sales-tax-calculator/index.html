Sales Tax Calculator

Calculate net, tax and gross totals for multiple line items. Choose from 30 countries. The tax type, currency and standard rate load automatically where one nationwide rate applies.

Use the tool

Choose one currency for the calculation. Country rates are standard reference rates for most taxable goods and services; reduced, zero-rated and exempt items may differ.

Select a country to load its tax type, currency, standard rate and official tax authority.

Checking the latest EU VAT rates…

Standard rates are reference values. Reduced rates, product categories, exemptions and regional taxes may differ. Brazil, Canada and the United States require a locally applicable rate. India and China have multiple applicable rate bands; confirm the product or service category before calculating.

How to use

  1. Add a line item for each product or service.
  2. Enter its amount, choose whether it is net or gross, and set the tax rate.
  3. Calculate to see net, tax and gross totals for every line and the complete invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as the VAT Calculator?

No. This tool totals multiple line items, each with its own rate and net-or-gross input basis.

Are the quick-pick rates correct for every product?

They are standard reference rates for most taxable supplies. Reduced, zero-rated and exempt categories may have different rates. Canada’s quick pick is federal GST only, and USA rates vary by state and locality. Check the linked official source.

Does this calculate business tax liability?

No. It is a general arithmetic aid, not tax or accounting advice.
