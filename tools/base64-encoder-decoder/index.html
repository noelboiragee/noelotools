Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode or decode UTF-8 text as Base64 directly in your browser.

Use the tool

How to use

  1. Enter plain text to encode, or paste Base64 text to decode.
  2. Select Encode to Base64 or Decode Base64.
  3. Review the output and select Copy output if you want to copy it.

Frequently asked questions

Does it support non-English text?

Yes. Encoding and decoding use UTF-8.

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is an encoding format, not encryption.

Does text leave my browser?

No. Conversion runs locally in the page.
