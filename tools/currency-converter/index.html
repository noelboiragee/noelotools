Frequently asked questions

Where do the rates come from?

The Frankfurter API distributes reference rates sourced from the European Central Bank.

Can I use the converter offline?

Previously saved rates may be used offline and are clearly labelled as cached. Without saved rates, the tool displays an error.

Are these rates suitable for trading?

No. They are indicative reference rates, not real-time trading quotes or bank transaction offers.

How often are rates updated?

The API publishes reference rates on business days; updates are not continuous.
