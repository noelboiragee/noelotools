About exchange rates

Rates are retrieved from a third-party exchange-rate service and may be updated periodically rather than continuously. The displayed conversion is indicative and may differ from bank, card-network, cash-exchange or money-transfer provider rates and fees.

Do not use this estimate as a guaranteed transaction quote. Confirm the final rate and fees with your bank or payment provider before exchanging money.

Exchange-rate data provided by ExchangeRate-API. This tool sends currency codes to the provider to retrieve public rate data; the amount entered is calculated locally in your browser.
