How to use

  1. Tap Start to begin timing.
  2. Tap Lap to record the current elapsed time.
  3. Tap Pause to stop; Start resumes from the saved elapsed time.
  4. Tap Reset to clear the stopwatch and lap list.

Frequently asked questions

Does the stopwatch stay accurate in a background tab?

Yes. It calculates elapsed time from performance.now() timestamps rather than counting timer ticks.

Does pausing clear laps?

No. Laps remain until you reset the stopwatch.

Is timing sent to a server?

No. Timing runs locally in your browser.
