About this estimate

This calculator is an educational estimate for a standard employee using the 2026 annual income-tax tariff and statutory social-insurance reference rates. It approximates taxable income using employee pension, health and care contributions plus a standard work-expense allowance.

It does not reproduce official monthly payroll withholding. Tax class, spouse income, children-related tax allowances, private insurance, special deductions, bonuses, one-off payments and the solidarity surcharge can change the result. The actual health-insurance additional rate also depends on your insurer.

Use the result as an indicative estimate, not as payroll, tax or legal advice. For an exact payslip calculation, use an official German payroll calculator or consult a qualified tax professional.

Reference data: German Income Tax Act §32a · Federal Ministry of Health · German Pension Insurance
