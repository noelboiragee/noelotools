How to use

  1. Choose a 30- or 60-second test.

  3. Review WPM, accuracy and error count when time ends.

Frequently asked questions

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute, conventionally estimated as five typed characters per word.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is correct characters divided by all typed characters, expressed as a percentage.

Can I restart a test?

Yes. Restart creates a new sample and resets the timer and statistics.
