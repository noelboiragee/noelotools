Adult BMI reference ranges

These commonly used adult screening ranges are general reference points, not a judgement about a person’s health.

  • Below 18.5Underweight range
  • 18.5–24.9Healthy-weight range
  • 25.0–29.9Overweight range
  • 30.0 and aboveObesity range

What BMI cannot tell you

BMI does not directly measure body fat or distinguish fat from muscle. It does not account fully for age, pregnancy, ethnicity, body composition, or where fat is carried. A clinician should interpret BMI alongside other health information.
