Your PDF is processed on your device and is not uploaded to NoeloTools. An internet connection is needed to load the compression libraries.

Which option should you choose?

  • Text Preservation: Best for documents where you need to select, search or copy text.
  • Maximum Compression: Useful for image-heavy PDFs when reducing file size is the priority.

Results depend on the original PDF. Image-based compression may reduce clarity and removes searchable text and some interactive features. Keep your original file.
