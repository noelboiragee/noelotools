Important information

Net salary depends on more than the country: tax year, tax class or filing status, region, age, children, health insurance, pension scheme and other deductions can all affect the result.

The available Germany model is a simplified 2026 estimate, not an official payroll calculation. Other country models are not yet enabled. Do not use this tool as tax, payroll or legal advice.
