Germany model: tax year 2026

This is a simplified estimate for 2026. The implementation uses a basic tax-free allowance of €12,348, an annual work-expense allowance of €1,230, and estimated contribution ceilings of €5,812.50 per month for health and long-term care insurance and €8,450 per month for pension and unemployment insurance.

  • Pension insurance: employee share 9.3% up to the pension contribution ceiling.
  • Unemployment insurance: employee share 1.3% up to the pension contribution ceiling.
  • Health insurance: employee share is half of 14.6% plus the entered insurer additional rate, up to the health contribution ceiling.
  • Long-term care insurance: estimated employee rate 1.8% for parents or 2.4% for childless people outside Saxony; 1.3% or 2.3% respectively in Saxony.
  • Income tax: simplified progressive 2026 formula using the tax brackets encoded in this calculator; church tax is applied at the selected 0%, 8% or 9% of estimated income tax.

The estimated taxable income subtracts the work-expense allowance and estimated employee pension, health and care contributions. It does not reproduce every official tax, allowance, surcharge, special case or payroll rule. Treat the output as an indicative estimate, not a payslip or tax filing calculation.

Important information

Net salary depends on more than the country: tax year, tax class or filing status, region, age, children, health insurance, pension scheme and other deductions can all affect the result.

The available Germany model is a simplified 2026 estimate, not an official payroll calculation. Other country models are not yet enabled. Do not use this tool as tax, payroll or legal advice.

Cross-country methodology reference: OECD Taxing Wages 2026. Country-specific rules will be documented individually as each model is released.
